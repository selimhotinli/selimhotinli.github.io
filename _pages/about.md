<div class="hero-section">
  <h1 class="hero-name">Selim C. Hotinli</h1>
  <p class="hero-tagline">Cosmologist probing the origin and contents of the Universe through the CMB and large-scale structure</p>
  <p class="hero-position">P. J. E. Peebles Fellow, Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics</p>
</div>

My research sits at the intersection of the cosmic microwave background and large-scale structure. I develop techniques to combine these datasets, using the CMB as a cosmic backlight to probe the contents, evolution, and fundamental physics of the Universe.

Previously: Horizon Fellow at Johns Hopkins, PhD at Imperial College London ([President's Scholar](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/pg/fees-and-funding/scholarships/presidents-phd-scholarships)). Full publication list at [InspireHEP](https://inspirehep.net/authors/1635388?ui-citation-summary=true).
